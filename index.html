Toward a Life Without Daily Insulin

BioPi is developing an implantable bioartificial pancreas that protects insulin-producing cells while enabling oxygen and nutrient exchange—aiming to restore natural blood-sugar regulation.

Explore the science Partner with us

Our Innovation

An immune-shielded micro-bioreactor that houses functional insulin-producing cells (islets). Our design combines a selective diffusion membrane with an oxygenation strategy to sustain long-term cell viability and responsiveness.

Immune protection

Selective barrier blocks immune attack while allowing glucose/insulin transport.

Smart oxygenation

Hybrid matrix and geometry enhance oxygen supply to encapsulated cells.

Physiologic response

Cells sense glucose changes and release insulin dynamically.

Replaceable module

Designed for minimally invasive replacement and monitoring.

Why it matters

  • Reduce hypoglycemia risk and glycemic variability
  • Alleviate daily burden of insulin dosing and carb counting
  • Potential for durable, physiologic glucose control

How it works

A seamless chain from cell sourcing to glucose-responsive insulin release.

1. Therapeutic cells

Islet or beta-like cells prepared under GMP conditions.

2. Encapsulation

Cells housed inside a selective, biocompatible bioreactor.

3. Oxygen & nutrients

Diffusion-optimized architecture sustains viability and function.

4. Glucose-responsive insulin

Physiologic insulin release in response to blood glucose changes.
