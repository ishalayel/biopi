BioPi

Towards a Life Without Insulin Therapy

For Type 1 Diabetes Patients

BioPi is developing an implantable bioartificial pancreas.

  • BioPi is developing an implantable bioartificial pancreas, designed to restore natural blood sugar regulation for patients with type 1 diabetes, without insuline injections. Our device encapsulates insulin-producing cells inside a protective bioreactor that allows oxygen and nutrients to pass while shielding the cells from the immune system.
