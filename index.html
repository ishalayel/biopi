Toward a Life Without Daily Insulin

BIOPI is developing a breakthrough bioartificial pancreas that mimics the natural function of the human organ. By protecting insulin-producing cells and ensuring continuous oxygen and nutrient supply, our device aims to deliver long-term, autonomous glucose regulation, without the need for daily insuline injections or lifelong immunosuppressive drugs.

Our Innovation

We are developing a next-generation bioartificial pancreas: an immune-shielded bioreactor that houses functional insulin-producing cells (islets). Its unique design combines a selective diffusion membrane with an oxygenation strategy to ensure long-term survival and responsiveness of cells.

Immune protection

Selective barrier blocks immune attack while allowing natural glucose/insulin exchange.

Smart oxygenation

Hybrid matrix and optimized geometry deliver continuous oxygen to encapsulated cells.

Physiologic response

Cells sense glucose changes and release insulin automatically.

Replaceable module

Engineered for minimally invasive replacement.

Why it matters

  • Reduce hypoglycemia risk and stabilize blood-sugar
  • Free patients from daily insuline injections
  • Provide durable, physiologic glucose control

How it works

From cell preparation to natural insuline release.

1. Therapeutic cells

Insulin-producing islets (or stem cell–derived beta cells) are prepared under strict GMP standards.

2. Encapsulation

Cells are placed inside a biocompatible, immune-shielding bioreactor that protects them while allowing nutrient and hormone exchange.

3. Oxygen & nutrients

A diffusion-optimized design ensures continuous oxygenation and nutrient supply, keeping the cells alive and functional long term.

4. Glucose-responsive insulin

The cells sense rising blood sugar and release insulin naturally, just like a healthy pancreas !
