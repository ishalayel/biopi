Our Innovation

Our device encapsulates insulin-producing cells inside a protective bioreactor, shielding them from the immune system while allowing oxygen and nutrients to pass.

This breakthrough technology aims to provide a long-term solution, freeing patients from the daily burden of insulin injections.
