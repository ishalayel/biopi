Toward a Life Without Daily Insulin

Over 500,000 people worldwide live with unstable Type 1 Diabetes. BIOPI is developing a breakthrough bioartificial pancreas that encapsulates insulin-producing cells in patented 3D hydrogels, providing long-term, autonomous glucose regulation without immunosuppressants or daily injections.

Our Innovation

Why BIOPI?

We are developing a bioartificial pancreas: an implantable bioreactor that encapsulates insulin-secreting cells using our unique, patented 3D hydrogel fabrication processes. This creates an optimal environment for cell survival, isolates them from the immune system, and enables the use of non-human origin cells for broader accessibility.

Immune Protection

Hydrogel barrier blocks immune attacks while allowing glucose/insulin diffusion.

Smart Oxygenation

Optimized 3D matrix ensures continuous oxygen and nutrient supply.

Physiologic Response

Cells detect glucose changes and secrete insulin autonomously.

Long-Term Durability

Designed for sustained function and minimally invasive replacement.

Impact

  • Autonomous insulin regulation without daily injections
  • No need for immunosuppressants post-implantation
  • Long-term bioreactor function for improved quality of life
  • Reduces hypoglycemia risks and stabilizes blood sugar

Disclaimer: Investigational device. Not approved for sale. Currently in pre-clinical phase with a functional prototype.

How It Works

1. Therapeutic Cells

Insulin-producing islets, potentially from non-human sources, prepared under GMP standards.

2. Encapsulation

Cells encapsulated in biocompatible, patented 3D hydrogels for immune isolation.

3. Oxygen & Nutrients

Optimized hydrogel matrix ensures sustained supply of oxygen and nutrients.

4. Glucose-Responsive Insulin

Cells sense blood glucose and release insulin naturally, restoring autonomous regulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Unlike traditional islet transplants that require immunosuppressants and human donors, BIOPI's bioartificial pancreas uses patented 3D hydrogels to encapsulate cells, protecting them from the immune system while allowing non-human cell sources for scalability.

We have a functional prototype in the pre-clinical phase, with ongoing tests and partnerships. Clinical trials are planned following successful pre-clinical results.

By providing autonomous glucose regulation, eliminating daily injections, and avoiding immunosuppressant side effects, BIOPI aims to enhance life expectancy and daily freedom.
